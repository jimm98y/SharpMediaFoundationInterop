sh_hrir_order_1.wav, sh_hrir_order_2.wav and sh_hrir_order_3.wav are Google VR's head-related impulse responses for
binaural decoding of ambisonics, of the first, second and third order - one channel of each of the sound field's, in
ACN order, of the left ear - as Omnitone carries them (https://github.com/GoogleChrome/omnitone, src/resources),
unchanged. They are based on the SADIE binaural measurements:

https://www.york.ac.uk/sadie-project/GoogleVRSADIE.html
